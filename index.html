Our Story

HAWIAD PRODUCTS CANADA INC. is a registered importation and Distribution Company established in 2021, importing the best quality ethnic food products from around the world. We are committed to providing our customers with authentic, great-tasting products that remind them of home.

We thoroughly check the quality of our goods, working only with reliable suppliers/manufacturers around the world so that you only receive the best quality product. The interests of our customers are always top priority for us, so we hope you will enjoy our products as much as we enjoy making them available to you.
